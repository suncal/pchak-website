01 — Mission Context Why finance data decides outcomes

Your numbers live in five systems. Your leadership wants one truth — without giving up Excel.

Most finance teams don't have a reporting problem; they have a plumbing problem. The ERP holds the ledger, the CRM holds the pipeline, payroll holds headcount, and the banks hold cash — so every month-end becomes a week of exports, copy-paste, and version-control anxiety before anyone can answer a simple question.

Tools like Datarails solve this by keeping Excel as the front end and connecting it to a governed, central source of truth. But the tool is only half the job: the value comes from disciplined integration, data mapping, and reconciliation — making sure the connected numbers tie back to the ledger before anyone trusts a dashboard built on them.

That engineering discipline is what we bring. We implement and support Datarails and similar FP&A platforms as an independent consultancy — we work for your finance team, not for any software vendor.

03 — How It Works

Four phases. No surprises.

Phase 01

Discovery

1–2 weeks: we review your systems and reporting needs, then deliver a data-mapping plan, dashboard specifications, and a fixed quote.

Phase 02

Connect & Map

We connect each source system and map your chart of accounts, entities, currencies, and dimensions into one consistent structure.

Phase 03

Validate

We reconcile the connected data against your ledger for recent closed months — numbers earn trust before dashboards are built on them.

Phase 04

Dashboards & Handover

We build the reports, train your team, and leave a runbook — so the system keeps working after we step back.

Timeline: Most projects take 6–10 weeks, depending on the number of entities and systems.
