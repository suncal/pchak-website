01 — The Problem Why most outbound fails

A list of names is not a pipeline. A reason to talk, sent to the right person, is.

Most lead generation sells volume: thousands of contacts, generic templates, and a dashboard full of "opens." Decision-makers ignore it, and your domain pays the price.

We work the other way. Every account is researched by hand: why it fits your profile, a dated trigger that makes now a good time, and a named decision-maker. Messages are short, specific and approved by you before anything is sent.

You only count a meeting when it was held, with the right person, and you confirmed it.

04 — Qualified Meeting

What counts, in writing.

A meeting counts toward the target only when all four are true. The morning after each meeting, you get a one-click email to confirm it.

The meeting was actually held. Cancelled or missed meetings don't count.
It was with a decision-maker or direct influencer for what you sell.
The company fits the profile we agreed on before the pilot started.
The prospect knew the meeting was about your company and agreed to it.
