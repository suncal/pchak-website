01 — Context Why Vena projects succeed or stall

Vena keeps planning in Excel. The value is in the model and the data.

Vena gives finance teams a governed planning platform inside Excel. Getting value from it depends on clean integrations and a well-designed data model.

We focus on integrations, data models, planning templates and reconciliation, so budgets, forecasts and reports run on trusted numbers.

We work as an independent consultancy, alongside Vena's own onboarding where that applies.

03 — How It Works

Four phases. No surprises.

Phase 01

Discovery

1–2 weeks: we review your systems and reporting needs, then deliver a plan, specifications and a fixed quote.

Phase 02

Connect & Map

We connect each source system and map accounts, entities, currencies and dimensions into one consistent structure.

Phase 03

Validate

We reconcile against your ledger for recent closed months, so numbers earn trust before reports are built on them.

Phase 04

Deliver & Handover

We build the reports, train your team and leave a runbook, with your sign-off at every phase.
