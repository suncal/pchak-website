01 — Context Why Business Central projects succeed or stall

Business Central runs the books. Reporting still runs on copy and paste.

Business Central is a strong ERP for growing companies, and it sits inside the Microsoft stack your team already uses. Getting reliable reporting out of it is where most teams get stuck.

We focus on integrations, reporting and consolidation: connecting Business Central to the rest of your systems and turning the ledger into dashboards leadership actually uses.

We also connect Business Central to FP&A platforms such as Datarails, Vena and Cube for planning, forecasting and board reporting.

03 — How It Works

Four phases. No surprises.

Phase 01

Discovery

1–2 weeks: we review your systems and reporting needs, then deliver a plan, specifications and a fixed quote.

Phase 02

Connect & Map

We connect each source system and map accounts, entities, currencies and dimensions into one consistent structure.

Phase 03

Validate

We reconcile against your ledger for recent closed months, so numbers earn trust before reports are built on them.

Phase 04

Deliver & Handover

We build the reports, train your team and leave a runbook, with your sign-off at every phase.
